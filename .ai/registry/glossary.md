# Glossary

The vocabulary the whole system reasons in. One term, one meaning, one spelling.

Rows are written by agents and approved by a human at merge, under CODEOWNERS (RULE-01, ADR-007).
**A new term is written at PLAN**, by `tech-lead-design`, for each term the ticket's `glossary_owed`
names, and ships in that ticket's pull request — ADR-047. `/triage` names the owed terms and writes
no row here.

*Amended 2026-09-29 by ADR-047. Read: "Human-only, per RULE-01. An agent that needs a term added
stops with `gate: BLOCKED` and states it in `blocking_reason`." — stale since ADR-007 exempted
glossary rows, and contradicted by every row added since.*

**Why this is a registry file and not a wiki page.** Agents name things from this list. A term with
two spellings becomes two field names, two DTO shapes, and a lint exemption, and the divergence is
only visible three tickets later. `.ai/standards/coding-standards.md` forbids abbreviations that are
not defined here, which is the mechanism that makes the list load-bearing rather than decorative.

**English is the identifier; Vietnamese is what the team says.** Code, field names and artifacts use
the term in the first column. The Vietnamese phrase in the second column is what the same thing is
called in conversation and in the interface, recorded here so the two never drift into being
different concepts.

## Terms

| Term | Means | Not to be confused with |
|------|-------|-------------------------|
| **Entry** | *đăng ký* — one declaration that one member will be away or remote, over one date or a run of consecutive dates. The unit everything else counts, approves and displays. | A leave request in the HR system. An entry is a plan announced to the team; it carries no employment consequence. |
| **PTO** | *nghỉ* — the member is not working. | Any distinction between paid and unpaid, or any quota. Neither is modelled — see charter refusal 1. |
| **WFH** | *làm việc từ xa* — the member **is working**, but not at the office. | PTO. A WFH member is available; this is the single most costly confusion in the domain, because both reduce office presence and only one reduces capacity. |
| **Portion** | *thời lượng* — how much of a day one entry covers: `full`, `am`, or `pm`. One entry carries exactly one, and it applies to **every** date in the entry's range (INV-06) — a five-day `pm` entry is five afternoons. | Hours. Three values, no finer resolution. Also not a per-day value: a trip leaving Wednesday afternoon and returning Monday morning is up to three entries, not one. |
| **Tentative** | *chưa chốt* — a flag on an entry meaning the member may still change it. Displayed to everyone and counted in every calculation; it differs only visually. | Approval status. These are two independent axes: a tentative entry can be approved, and a non-tentative entry can be pending. Collapsing them into one field is the modelling mistake this row exists to prevent. |
| **Approval status** | *trạng thái duyệt* — `pending`, `approved`, or `rejected`. Set by an admin or a manager (ADR-035), and **PTO and WFH alike** — decided by the operator on 2026-08-31, so `status` is meaningful on every row and INV-03 has no exception. | Tentative. See above. |
| **Absence count** | *số người vắng* — the derived number of people away on a given date, over that date's `pending` and `approved` entries **whose member was still on the team that date**: 1 per `full`, 0.5 per `am` or `pm`, PTO and WFH alike. **Rejected entries are excluded**, and so are entries of a member removed on or before the date (ADR-013). | A headcount of people. It is a decimal, it is derived and never stored, and it has exactly one definition anywhere in the system (INV-04). Not a function of the date's entries alone — it needs the roster. |
| **Threshold** | *ngưỡng* — the share of team size above which a date is overloaded. Default 50%, configurable by an admin. It multiplies the team's **current** member count, evaluated when the number is read — so a past date can change between overloaded and normal when somebody joins or leaves. | A hard limit. Nothing is ever refused because of it — charter refusal 6. Not a historical figure: membership as it stood on the date in question is deliberately not stored. |
| **Overloaded day** | *ngày quá tải* — a date whose absence count exceeds the threshold. | A blocked day. It is a warning and a visual state, nothing more. |
| **Holiday** | *ngày lễ* — a public non-working day in Vietnam, plus the swap and compensatory days the government announces each year, which an admin enters. | A day someone took off. Holidays belong to the calendar, not to any member. |
| **Bridge day** | *ngày cầu* — a working day sandwiched between a holiday and a weekend, computed from the holiday calendar. | A holiday. It is an ordinary working day that is merely very likely to be requested. |
| **Team** | The group whose members see one another's entries and against whose size the threshold is computed. | An organisation or department. Many teams exist since ADR-039; under [ADR-059](decisions/ADR-059-calechip-becomes-multi-tenant-system-org-team.md) every team sits inside one org, and a member may belong to several teams of that org. *Until 2026-10-13 this read "Exactly one team exists in v1; multiple teams are deferred, not refused."* |
| **Member** | The role that creates and edits its **own** entries and reads the entries of everyone on the teams it belongs to. *Until 2026-10-13: "and reads everyone's" — written when there was one team (ADR-059).* | A user account in general. Every person in the system is a member; some are additionally managers or admins. |
| **Manager** | A member who may also approve or reject **another** member's entry — and nothing else a member cannot do. Added 2026-09-12 by [ADR-035](decisions/ADR-035-a-third-role-manager-decides-entries-and-nothing-else.md). | An admin with fewer buttons. A manager maintains no calendar, changes no roster and no threshold, edits nobody's entry but their own, and may not decide their own. |
| **Admin** | A member who can also approve and reject **including their own entries**, maintain the holiday calendar, invite people, and set the threshold. Since ADR-035 the approve half is shared with **Manager**; the rest is the admin's alone. | An owner or a billing role. Neither exists. |
| **Event** | *sự kiện* — a gathering one member announces, over one date or a run of dates with no times, for a scope: their own team, named people, or every team. Added 2026-09-29 by [ADR-045](decisions/ADR-045-charter-refusals-4-and-5-are-lifted-for-events.md). | An entry. An event creates no entry, is not in the absence count and is not drawn on the calendar grids; attending one says nothing about whether a person is away. |
| **Attendee** | *người tham gia* — a member whose request to join an event is approved, or who joined an event that needs no approval. Only attendees count against capacity; a pending request does not. The event's creator is not one unless they join. | A member counted in the absence count. The two never touch. |
| **Invitation** | *lời mời* — naming a person on an event whose scope is named people, which lets them see it and request to join it. It is not a pre-approval: in an approval-required event an invitee waits like anyone else. | Joining the product. The `TEA` group's *invitations*, and ADR-033's sign-up path, mean letting a person onto the board; this word means asking a person who is already on it to a gathering. |
| **Capacity** | *số chỗ* — an optional upper bound on an event's attendees, set by its creator. It may never be set below the current number of attendees, and it must hold under concurrent joins and approvals. | The threshold. Capacity refuses a join when full; the threshold refuses nothing (charter refusal 6). |
| **Guest link** | *link cho khách* — the unguessable link an event's creator, or an admin, creates by opening the event to guests. Anyone holding it reads that one event and its attendees' names without an account, whatever the event's scope; nothing lists or indexes it, and closing the event to guests stops it. Added 2026-10-09 by [ADR-052](decisions/ADR-052-an-event-can-be-opened-to-guests-without-an-account.md) decisions 1–3, from `2026-10-08-a-gathering-cannot-include-anyone-without-an-account.md` Q2, Q3, Q9 and F2. | The event's own address inside the product (`/events/:id`), which needs a sign-in and follows the event's scope. A guest's manage link (`EVT-07`, ADR-052 decision 6), which is one guest's secret for their own registration. An invitation — a guest link names nobody. |
| **Guest** | *khách* — a person without an account who registers for an event opened to guests, through its guest link, with a name and an email. A guest takes a seat and follows the event's capacity, approval mode and registration deadline exactly as a member does, and manages their registration — its status, and cancelling it — through a secret manage link shown once on screen. A guest's name is readable by everyone who can read the event; their email by the event's creator and admins only. One registration per email per event; guest data is kept indefinitely. Added 2026-10-09 by [ADR-052](decisions/ADR-052-an-event-can-be-opened-to-guests-without-an-account.md) decisions 4–9, from `2026-10-08-a-gathering-cannot-include-anyone-without-an-account.md` Q5, Q6, Q7, Q8 and F1. | A **Member** — a guest has no account, no team and no `public.member` row, and never enters an absence count (ADR-052 decision 9). An **Attendee** in this glossary's sense, which is a member — though an attending guest counts against **Capacity** like one. A pending sign-up, who is asking to join the product, not an event. |
| **Notification** | *thông báo* — one row the database writes for one person when something happens to an event that concerns them: a new event for their team or for everyone, being invited by name, their join request approved, rejected or removed, an event they take part in changed or cancelled, or — for its creator — a join request or a withdrawal. Only ever addressed to someone who can read the event at that moment; read and marked read by that person alone; never written for the person who caused it. Added 2026-10-08 by [ADR-050](decisions/ADR-050-in-app-notifications-for-events-are-written-by-the-database.md), from `2026-10-08-a-person-learns-about-an-event-only-by-going-to-look.md` Q2. | Email about an event (`EVT-05`, ADR-051) — a notification is in-app only. The overload warning — that is computed for a date and stored nowhere. A reminder — none exists; nothing is written on a schedule (ADR-050 decision 6). |
| **Email invitation** | *lời mời qua email* — one email address entered for one **public** event by its creator or an admin, and the at most one mail that follows it. Entering it opens the event to guests if it is not open. An address that is not an approved member's is mailed the event's guest link; an approved member's is mailed a link into the app, only if their event email is on. Each address is invited at most once per event; the inviter is told which addresses were invited and which were already invited, never which belong to members. The list is read by the event's creator and admins only, and kept indefinitely. Added 2026-10-12 by [ADR-055](decisions/ADR-055-an-entered-address-is-mailed-an-invitation-to-a-public-event.md), from `2026-10-12-a-person-outside-the-product-learns-of-a-public-event-only-by-a-forwarded-link.md` Q1, Q2, Q6, Q9, Q11 and F1. | An **Invitation** — naming a member on a named-people event, which lets them see it; an email invitation names an address, not a member, and grants nothing to read. The **Guest link** itself, which names nobody. A **Guest** — an invited address becomes one only by registering through the link. A **Notification** — an email invitation writes no in-app row. |
| **Limited people** | *limited people* — the operator's own words, kept in English in conversation (2026-10-14). The label a person reads for the event scope stored as `named`, which was shown as *Named people* until then: the event is readable by the members named on it, and not by the rest of the creator's team. Since ADR-061 an *Own team* event becomes one when its creator or an admin invites by email a member of another team: that team as it is at that moment, plus the new person. Added 2026-10-14 by EVT-12, from `2026-10-14-a-team-or-named-event-cannot-invite-anyone-by-email.md` § *Evidence* (the relabelling) and [ADR-061](decisions/ADR-061-invitations-reach-every-event-scope-and-a-signed-in-reader-leaves-the-guest-page.md) decision 2 (the conversion). | The database value and every identifier, which stay `named` (`event_scope`, `EventScope`, `event-scope-named`). *Named people* in older rows and documents, including **Event** and **Invitation** above — the same scope under its former label. *Own team*, which follows the team as it changes; a *Limited people* event does not. |
| **Sanitised description** | *mô tả (đã lọc)* — an event's description as stored and as shown: HTML, stored as text, written by the one editor on create and edit, and on every surface that shows it passed through the one sanitiser against one allow-list before it reaches the reader — bold, italic, underline, strikethrough, H2/H3, bulleted and numbered lists, links, blockquote, text colour and alignment; `img` with a `data:` image source joins with `EVT-11`. The sanitising that protects a reader is the render's; the same sanitise before saving is defence in depth only, because the API can be called directly (ADR-005). Added 2026-10-12 by [ADR-056](decisions/ADR-056-an-event-description-is-sanitised-html-from-one-editor.md) decisions 1–3, from `2026-10-12-an-event-description-cannot-carry-formatting-or-a-picture.md` Q1, Q3 and Q5. | Plain text — anything that reads the column as text (a search, an excerpt, a future email) must strip tags first (ADR-056 § Consequences). An authorization control — sanitising decides which markup a reader receives, never whether they receive the row (ADR-005). The description in event email, which does not carry it (Q7-A). |
| **Quarantine** | *cách ly* (TODO(verify): the team's own spoken word is not recorded in the idea file or ADR-060; this is the plain translation) — an end-to-end test that is failing, marked `test.fixme` so the suite stops running it, and allowed only while all three of these hold: a comment on the line names a tracking ticket, `docs/ops/e2e-quarantine.md` has a row for the test with its suspected cause, first failing PR and ticket, and that row carries an expiry no more than 30 days out. Past its expiry the row is fixed, deleted with a reason, or re-dated by the operator. An exception to the refusal of skipped tests in `.ai/standards/testing-standards.md`, and nothing wider. Added 2026-10-13 by [ADR-060](decisions/ADR-060-a-failing-e2e-test-may-be-quarantined-with-a-register-and-a-ticket.md) option A, from `2026-10-13-a-red-main-merges-unnoticed-and-no-release-is-built-or-deployed.md` § 5 Phase 1. | A **flaky** test — it is not quarantined: it keeps running under the existing bounded CI retry and gets a register row marked *flaky*, so the retry is visible (ADR-060 § Decision). A skipped test (`test.skip`), which `testing-standards.md` still refuses. Deleting the test (ADR-060 option C), which loses what it asserted. Fixing the test, which is the tracking ticket's work, not the quarantine's. |
| **Task** | *task* — the operator's own word (2026-10-12). One item a member writes on their own list for one date: a title, an optional short plain-text note, and exactly two states, done and not done. It is read and written by its owner only — no teammate, manager or admin, through any token the app issues. It may carry one optional **Time of day**, empty by default, and carries no estimate, duration, end time or hours. Created for today or a later date; a past task can still be read, ticked, edited and moved. Added 2026-10-15 by TSK-01, from [ADR-057](decisions/ADR-057-a-task-is-readable-and-writable-by-its-owner-only.md) decisions 1, 5 and 6 and `2026-10-12-a-member-has-nowhere-to-plan-their-own-day-and-an-absence-hides-what-was-due.md` answer 1, F1, F2, Q8 and Q13. *Amended 2026-10-18 by TSK-06, from [ADR-062](decisions/ADR-062-a-task-may-carry-an-optional-time-of-day.md) decisions 1 and 6. Read: "and it carries no time of day, estimate, duration or hours."* | An **Entry** — a task is not a declaration of absence, is in no **Absence count**, and is never drawn as an entry. A tracker ticket — nothing syncs with ClickUp, Jira or any tracker (answer 6). A timesheet line — charter refusal 3. |
| **Time of day** | *giờ* — the operator's *"có thể thêm giờ hoặc không, default là không có giờ. coi như giờ là là extra info"* (Q2). An optional wall-clock time, `HH:MM` on a 24-hour clock in whole minutes, on a **Task**, read in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh` like every date in the app and carrying no zone of its own. Empty by default. It is shown, and it orders a day's list — not-done before done, then timed tasks by time, then untimed by creation (Q5) — and it does nothing else. Set, changed and cleared by the task's owner when adding or editing; a move to another date keeps it. Added 2026-10-18 by TSK-06, from [ADR-062](decisions/ADR-062-a-task-may-carry-an-optional-time-of-day.md) decisions 1 to 5 and `2026-10-18-the-task-page-shows-one-day-at-a-time-and-a-task-has-no-time.md` Q2 and Q5. | A deadline — a task is **Overdue** by its date alone, so a 09:00 task is not overdue at 10:00 the same day (ADR-057 decision 7). A reminder — nothing fires at that time (Q7, a separate idea). A duration, estimate or hours worked — charter refusal 3, ADR-062 decision 6. An instant — it names no zone and is not converted. |
| **Overdue** | *quá hạn* — said of a **Task** whose date is before today and which is not done (F1, *"qua ngày hôm sau thì gọi là quá hạn"*). "Today" is the calendar date in `Asia/Ho_Chi_Minh`. A done task is never overdue, and a task stops being overdue the moment it is ticked or moved to today or later. Added 2026-10-15 by TSK-01, from [ADR-057](decisions/ADR-057-a-task-is-readable-and-writable-by-its-owner-only.md) decision 7; the clock is TSK-01 01-plan.md § 1 decision 1. | An **Overloaded day**, which is about the team's absence count, not one person's list. A refusal — an overdue task can still be ticked, edited, moved or deleted, and nothing is blocked (charter refusal 6). |
| **Org** | *tổ chức* — the tenant boundary in `public.org` above **Team**, with fully isolated data, its own holiday calendar and configuration, and an immutable URL slug `org_code` (`/{org_code}`) that may never be a reserved word. Every **Member**, **Team**, **Entry**, **Holiday**, **Event** and related data row belongs to exactly one org (`org_id`), enforced at the database by composite foreign keys. Added 2026-10-27 by MIR-10, from [ADR-059](decisions/ADR-059-calechip-becomes-multi-tenant-system-org-team.md) decision 1 and [ADR-070](decisions/ADR-070-the-org-and-multi-team-membership-schema.md) decision 2. | A **Team** — an org holds one or more teams, and a member of an org may belong to several of its teams. The whole deployment (*system*), which sits above every org and is administered from a separate system panel (MIR-18). |
| **Org public** | *Org public* — the single shared-tenant **Org** row (`is_shared_tenant = true`, `name = 'Org public'`, `org_code = 'shared'`) that holds free teams not belonging to a dedicated licensed org, and into which MIR-12 backfills all existing teams and members. Administered by `system_admin`, who alone sets its holidays; every cross-team or org-wide visibility surface (member directory, `public`-scope events and event notifications) stays within a single team when `is_shared_tenant = true` (MIR-15). Added 2026-10-27 by MIR-10, from [ADR-059](decisions/ADR-059-calechip-becomes-multi-tenant-system-org-team.md) decision 2 and [ADR-070](decisions/ADR-070-the-org-and-multi-team-membership-schema.md) decision 2. | A dedicated **Org** (`is_shared_tenant = false`), where org-wide features span all teams of that org. The event scope `public` (`event_scope`), which inside Org public is restricted to the event's own team rather than spanning every team in the shared tenant. |
| **Membership** | *thành viên nhóm* — one row in `public.membership` placing one **Member** (`member_id`) onto one **Team** (`team_id`) within the same **Org** (`org_id`), carrying a per-team role (`role_in_team`: `member` or `manager`), an `is_primary` flag (at most one active primary team per member), and a temporal window (`joined_at`, `left_at`). Leaving a team sets `left_at` rather than deleting the row, so **INV-04** and **INV-07** can evaluate whether a member was on a given team on a given date across all of the member's teams. Added 2026-10-27 by MIR-10, from [ADR-059](decisions/ADR-059-calechip-becomes-multi-tenant-system-org-team.md) decisions 1, 3 and 7 and [ADR-070](decisions/ADR-070-the-org-and-multi-team-membership-schema.md) decisions 3 and 5. | The **Member** account record (`public.member`) — a person has one `member` row in one org, and one `membership` row per team they join. The retired single-team column `member.team_id`, which MIR-12 backfills into `public.membership` and MIR-21 drops. |
